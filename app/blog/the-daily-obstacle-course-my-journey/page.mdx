# The Daily Obstacle Course: My Journey to University

As a Computer Science student at UMT, I usually focus on logic and solving problems. However, the path I take to university is not logical at all. This project helped me stop ignoring the problems around me.

On my way, I took photos of deep potholes, Uncovered sewage drain, and roads that were never finished. In images like
